# Daybook

A private daily tracker for Android: sleep, food, water, exercise, spending, subscriptions and your own habits, with XP, levels and a weekly boss to beat. There's no account, and everything stays on your phone.

## Install

1. **Download:** on your Android phone (Android 8 or later), tap **[Download Daybook](https://github.com/JosiahK28/daybook-releases/releases/latest/download/daybook.apk)**.
2. **Install:** open the downloaded file. If your phone asks, allow your browser or Files app to install apps, then tap **Install**.
3. **If Google Play Protect asks about it:** that's because Daybook isn't on the Play Store. You can choose to install it anyway.

## Updates

Daybook checks for new versions itself. When one is out you'll see **Update available**, with what's new; tap **Update**. The first time, Android asks you to let Daybook install its updates.

## Privacy

What you log never leaves your phone. The only thing Daybook does online is ask this page whether there's a newer version. You can turn that off in Settings → Updates.
