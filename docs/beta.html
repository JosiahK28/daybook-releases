Daybook beta guide

For testers. Daybook is a habit tracker that is also a dungeon game, built Australia-first. Short version: log your day, play the dungeon, back up now and then, tell me what broke.

Your first day

  1. Open Daybook and follow the first-run steps. Set your targets (sleep, energy, water, exercise) to numbers you actually want. Daybook is a tracking aid, not medical advice.
  2. Log one thing on Today: a glass of water is the quickest.
  3. Open Delve. Your logging lights the torch; you play the runs yourself.
  4. Allow notifications if you want reminders. Everything is optional.

Back up and restore

  • Back up: Settings, Data and trust, Export backup. Save the file somewhere you keep, like Drive or your computer.
  • Weekly backup: Settings, Data and trust, Weekly backup, Choose folder. Daybook keeps the newest four.
  • Restore: Settings, Data and trust, Restore from a backup. It replaces everything on the phone with the file. Daybook saves a copy of what it replaced first, but export a backup before you restore if you are unsure.

Installing and updating

  • Install from the download link on the main page. Android asks you to allow installs from your browser or file manager, once.
  • Play Protect may warn about an app it does not know. Tap More details, then Install anyway. Daybook is not on the Play Store.
  • Android has started checking who made apps installed from outside the Play Store. If Android shows a verification or "advanced" screen when you install, follow the steps it shows. Google explains the rules at developer.android.com/developer-verification. If you are stuck, send me a message.
  • Updates arrive inside the app and Android always asks you to confirm. Updates keep your data.

Uninstalling

Export a backup first, then remove Daybook like any app. This deletes everything stored in it. See the privacy policy for what, if anything, leaves your phone.

When something breaks

  1. Settings, Send feedback. Pick Bug, say what you did and what you expected, press Send. It goes to the developer privately.
  2. In Settings, Diagnostics, tap Copy diagnostics and paste it into your message. It holds the app version and phone model, not your logs.
  3. If the app crashed, say yes to the crash report when it asks. You can leave it off.

Known issues

  • Widgets and Quick Settings tiles have had less testing on real phones than the app itself. Tell me if a widget is cut off or blank.
  • The first time Daybook asks for notifications, some phones pause for a moment.
