Daybook

A habit tracker that's also a game. Log sleep, food, water, exercise and spending, and your real days power a pixel dungeon.

Download for Android

About 13 MB. Free. No account, no ads.

On a computer? Scan this with your phone's camera.

Installing

  1. Tap Download and open the file when it finishes.
  2. Android asks to allow installs from your browser. Allow it once.
  3. If Play Protect warns about an unknown developer, choose Install anyway. Daybook isn't on the Play Store yet.

What you get

  • Everything stays on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Reminders, a home-screen widget and Quick Settings tiles for fast logging.
  • Daily quests, a weekly boss and a dungeon you can play.
  • It updates itself from the same page.

Daybook is Android only for now.
